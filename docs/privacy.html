Kiddo Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

DromoMinds (“we”, “us”) built the Kiddo app (“Kiddo”) as a learning app for young children. This policy explains what information the app handles and how we treat it.

Summary

  • Kiddo is designed for kids to learn through play.
  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not show third-party ads in the app.
  • Progress and optional profile details are stored on the device, not on our servers.

Information stored on your device

Kiddo may store the following information locally on your phone or tablet using device storage (for example, app storage / AsyncStorage):

  • Optional child first name (for friendly voice prompts)
  • Optional birthday or age-related profile details entered by a parent or caregiver
  • Learning progress, stars, coins, gems, badges, and completed activities
  • App settings needed for the app to work

This information stays on the device unless you delete the app or clear app data. We do not automatically upload this information to DromoMinds servers.

Information we do not collect

Kiddo does not collect or require:

  • Email address, phone number, or social login
  • Precise location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, or microphone recordings
  • Payment information inside the app

Device features the app uses

  • Text-to-speech: on-device voice guidance during activities
  • Sound effects / music: in-app audio feedback
  • Haptic feedback: optional vibration on supported devices
  • Share: if you choose to share the app, your device’s normal share sheet is used
  • Links: the app may open the Google Play Store or our website when you tap related buttons

Children’s privacy

Kiddo is intended for young learners. Optional profile information should be entered by a parent or caregiver. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through online accounts because the app does not use accounts or server-side child profiles.

Third-party services

The app runs on your device and uses standard platform services provided by Google/Android (for example, app installation, updates, and device text-to-speech). Google Play’s own terms and privacy policies may apply when you download, update, or rate the app.

Data retention and deletion

Data stored by Kiddo remains on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app storage in your device settings.

Security

Because app data is stored locally on your device, please keep your device protected with a screen lock and only install apps from trusted sources such as the Google Play Store.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
